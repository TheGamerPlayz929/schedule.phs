Privacy & Safety

Effective August 30, 2026

Who we are

Poolesville Schedule and GradeViewer are independent, student-run services for the Poolesville High School community. They are not operated, sponsored, or approved by Poolesville High School, MCPS, Edupoint, or StudentVUE.

Schedule-site analytics

We keep anonymous totals for page category, device class, local day and hour, page views, and approximate visible time. These events contain no name, email, account, grades, StudentVUE data, IP address, user agent, or persistent identifier. Totals are deleted after 90 days.

Google Analytics and advertising trackers are not used. Our hosting and security providers still receive ordinary network information, such as an IP address, when your browser connects. The backend may use an IP briefly in memory for abuse prevention, but it is not added to analytics.

This setting is stored only in this browser. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track turn analytics off automatically.

GradeViewer

Your StudentVUE username and password pass through our protected backend and the StudentVUE connection only to sign in and request your information. We do not intentionally store or log the password. District session cookies are kept in server memory for up to eight hours or until logout.

Grades, attendance, schedules, documents, photos, and basic student details are displayed in your browser and are not intentionally written to our database. If you select Remember me, GradeViewer stores only your username in your browser for up to seven days. Use only an account you own.

Administrators and uploads

Approved administrators sign in with Google. We process their email, display name, profile image, session information, IP address, and actions for authentication and security. Admin sessions last up to eight hours; audit records and settings backups are limited to 180 days. Uploaded public images remain until an administrator deletes them.

When an administrator intentionally uses an AI tool, the selected text, settings, or images are sent to the configured AI provider to answer that request. Student GradeViewer records are not used for admin AI features or model training by this service.

Service providers

Firebase hosts the public pages, Render runs the backend, a private GitHub repository may store site settings and operational records, Google provides administrator sign-in, and MCPS/Edupoint provides StudentVUE. Each provider processes data under its own terms. We do not sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or create advertising profiles.

Children and location

The service is designed for a high-school community and is not directed to children under 13. A child under 13 should not use GradeViewer without a parent or guardian. The service is operated from the United States and intended primarily for the Poolesville community; laws outside Maryland may provide additional rights.

Your choices and rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, appeal a denied request, or ask a privacy question. We will verify requests before disclosing or deleting data and will not discriminate against you for making one. Because anonymous analytics cannot identify a person, we cannot retrieve one person’s aggregate events.

Contact emirbakir523@gmail.com or thegamerp929@gmail.com. For account safety, also change your StudentVUE password if you believe it was exposed.

Security and changes

We use encrypted connections, restricted administrator access, short-lived sessions, input limits, and security logging. No online service can promise perfect security. Material policy changes will be posted here with a new effective date; we will seek consent first if a new use legally requires it.
