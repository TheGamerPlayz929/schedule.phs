How Poolesville Schedule and GradeViewer handle your information.

Information we use

You can browse schedules and announcements without an account. Other features use the following information:

  • GradeViewer. Your StudentVUE username and password pass through our backend to sign you in to the district system. Your grades, attendance, schedule, documents, photo, and student details pass through the backend for display in your browser. We do not intentionally store or log your password or save your education records to our database.
  • Administrator access. Google provides approved administrators' account details, including email, name, and profile image. We keep session information, submitted site content, and records of administrative actions.
  • Support. If you contact us, we use your email address and message to respond.
  • Connection information. Hosting providers receive IP addresses and other technical details when serving requests. Our backend uses network information for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Administrator audit logs use a keyed, one-way network identifier instead of a raw IP address.

We use this information to operate the site, provide requested features, protect accounts, and respond to support or legal requests. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Analytics and browser storage

We collect first-party usage totals: page category, device class, page views, and approximate time a page is visible. These totals are grouped by day and hour. Analytics payloads contain no name, email, StudentVUE information, IP address, or persistent visitor identifier. Network requests still expose connection information to our hosting providers.

Analytics are always on. There is no opt-in or opt-out control. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or session replay. We do not currently respond to DNT or GPC browser signals.

Signed-in features use session cookies. Your browser also stores appearance preferences. If you select Remember me in GradeViewer, it saves your username for up to seven days, but not your password. You can remove saved preferences and usernames through your browser's site-data settings.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase Hosting to serve public pages, Render to run the backend, GitHub to store site content and operational records, Google Identity Services for administrator sign-in, and MCPS/Edupoint StudentVUE to retrieve the records you request. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

When administrators use an AI feature, the text or images submitted with that request are sent to the configured AI provider. GradeViewer education records are not used for these admin features. We do not use student records to train AI models.

Our use of Google API data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to address abuse or security incidents. If responsibility for the project changes, relevant operational records may pass to the new maintainers.

How long information is kept

  • GradeViewer sessions: district session information is held in server memory for up to eight hours, or until you log out.
  • Saved usernames: up to seven days in your browser when Remember me is selected.
  • Administrator sessions: up to eight hours.
  • Usage totals, audit logs, and settings backups: active records are limited to 90 days. Repository history and provider backups may retain earlier copies beyond that period.
  • Published content and uploaded images: retained until removed; earlier versions may remain in revision history.
  • Support messages: retained as needed to resolve and document the request.

MCPS controls retention of your original education records. Files you download remain on your device until you delete them.

Security

We use encrypted connections, restricted administrator access, short-lived sessions, and request limits. No online service can guarantee complete security. If you suspect your StudentVUE credentials were exposed, change your password through the district and contact us. Do not send passwords or school records by email.

Younger users

The service is intended for a high-school community and is not directed to children under 13. Children under 13 should not submit credentials or personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove information we hold.

Your choices and requests

You can use the schedule without signing in, log out of GradeViewer to end its session, and clear saved browser data at any time.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to information we hold about you, obtain a copy, request correction or deletion, or appeal a denied request. Email either contact below. We may need to verify your identity before acting and will explain any limitation or denial. We do not discriminate against you for making a privacy request.

We cannot identify your individual activity within aggregate usage totals. To correct or obtain your official education records, contact Poolesville High School or MCPS directly.
