Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions, contact us at emirbakir523@gmail.com.

1. What information do we collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

The schedule site itself requires no account and no sign-up. We collect personal information only when you choose to use a feature that needs it:

  • StudentVUE credentials. When you sign in to GradeViewer, your StudentVUE username and password pass through our protected backend to the district's StudentVUE connection so that it can sign you in and return your information. We do not intentionally store or log your password.
  • Education records. At your request, StudentVUE returns your grades, attendance, class schedule, documents, student photo, and basic student details. These are displayed in your browser and are not intentionally written to our database.
  • Administrator account details. Approved administrators sign in with Google. We process their email address, display name, profile image, and session information.
  • Content administrators submit. Announcements, schedule changes, site settings, and uploaded public images.
  • Correspondence. If you email us a question, a correction, or a privacy request, we process what you send us in order to answer it.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes. Use only a StudentVUE account you own.

Information automatically collected

In Short: Anonymous usage totals and ordinary technical information are collected automatically when you visit the Services.

The information collected automatically includes:

  • Anonymous usage totals. Page category, device class (mobile, tablet, or desktop), your local day and hour, page views, and approximate visible time on the page. These events contain no name, email, account, grades, StudentVUE data, IP address, user agent, cookie, or persistent identifier, and they cannot be tied to a person or a session.
  • Ordinary network information. Our hosting and security providers receive an IP address and similar connection details whenever your browser connects to them, as any web server does. Our backend may hold an IP address briefly in memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention. It is never added to analytics, and administrator audit records store only a keyed, one-way network identifier rather than a raw IP address.
  • Device preferences. Your browser stores your theme and appearance choices locally on your own device. If you select "Remember me" in GradeViewer, your browser stores only your username, for up to seven days. We cannot read these values from our servers.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google sign-in is used only to authenticate approved administrators.

2. How do we process your information?

In Short: We process your information to provide and operate the Services, to keep them secure, and to comply with law.

  • To provide the Services you request. To sign you in to StudentVUE and display your own records, and to show the schedule, announcements, and schedule changes.
  • To authenticate administrators and keep the site accountable. Administrator sign-in, session management, and an audit record of administrative actions.
  • To keep the Services secure and prevent abuse. Rate limiting, origin checks, and minimized security logging.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the Services. Using the anonymous totals described in section 4.
  • To respond to you. To answer questions, corrections, and privacy requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect the vital interests, rights, or safety of our users or others.

When an administrator intentionally uses an AI tool inside the admin panel, the selected text, settings, or images are sent to the configured AI provider to answer that specific request. Student GradeViewer records are never used for admin AI features, and no information from the Services is used by us to train AI models.

If the Services are ever transferred to new maintainers, information may be transferred as part of that handover, and this notice will be updated.

We do not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use it for targeted advertising, or create advertising profiles.

4. Do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

In Short: We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking technologies. Anonymous first-party analytics are always on and cannot identify you.

Analytics are always on. Every visit to a public page sends anonymous first-party usage totals to our own backend. There is no opt-in or opt-out control, because the events are anonymous by design and there is nothing personal to withhold. Each event records only the page category, the device class, your local day and hour, the event type, and approximate visible time. It contains no name, email, account, grades, StudentVUE data, IP address, user agent, cookie, advertising identifier, or persistent identifier, and no value that would let us recognize the same visitor twice. Because the totals are anonymous, they cannot be traced back to any individual, and they are deleted after 90 days.

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, web beacons, session-replay tools, fingerprinting, or any cross-site tracker. We do not permit third parties to place tracking technologies on the Services.

Cookies. The public schedule pages do not set analytics or advertising cookies. Cookies are used only where they are strictly necessary to operate a signed-in session: an HttpOnly session cookie for administrators, and a district session held in server memory while you use GradeViewer. Your own browser also stores appearance preferences and, if you select "Remember me," your GradeViewer username, in local storage on your device. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings, though doing so will reset your preferences.

5. How long do we keep your information?

In Short: We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes in this notice.

  • StudentVUE passwords: not intentionally stored or logged at any point.
  • GradeViewer district sessions: held in server memory for up to eight hours, or until you log out, whichever comes first.
  • Grades, attendance, schedules, documents, and student details: displayed in your browser and not intentionally written to our database.
  • Saved GradeViewer username: stored in your own browser for up to seven days if you select "Remember me."
  • Administrator sessions: up to eight hours.
  • Administrator audit records and settings backups: limited to 90 days.
  • Anonymous analytics totals: deleted after 90 days.
  • Uploaded public images and published site content: kept until an administrator deletes them.
  • Email correspondence: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and to show that we handled it.

When we have no ongoing need to process personal information, we delete or anonymize it, or securely store and isolate it from further processing until deletion is possible.

6. How do we keep your information safe?

In Short: We use organizational and technical safeguards, but no online service can promise perfect security.

We use encrypted connections, restricted and allowlisted administrator access, HttpOnly session cookies, short-lived sessions and sign-in handoffs, input limits, rate limits, origin checks, dependency review, and minimized security logging. Despite these safeguards, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise that unauthorized third parties will never defeat our security. You should access the Services only from a device and network you trust. If you believe your StudentVUE password may have been exposed, change it with the district and tell us.

8. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most web browsers, and some mobile operating systems and applications, include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or a Global Privacy Control ("GPC") setting that signals your preference not to have your online activity monitored. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized.

We do not currently respond to DNT or GPC browser signals. Our analytics are anonymous first-party totals that cannot identify, profile, or follow you across sites, and we do not sell or share personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is no sale, sharing, or targeted advertising for an opt-out preference signal to switch off. California law requires us to tell you how we respond to DNT signals, and this section is that disclosure. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow, we will update this notice.
